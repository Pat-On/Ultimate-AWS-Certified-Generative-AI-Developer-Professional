# 97. Section Intro

This section covers **Agentic AI**, meaning systems that don't just generate responses but **reason, plan, take action, collaborate** and integrate with real tools and data across your organization.

## What the section covers

- **LLM agents in Amazon Bedrock**, including hands-on creation of your first Bedrock agent.
- **Multi-agent workflows**, how agents coordinate, and how they use **short-term and long-term memory** to keep context and perform better.
- The **AWS-native agentic ecosystem**: **Strands Agents**, **AWS Agent Squad** and **AgentCore**, with a lab combining Strands agents and Agent Squad to orchestrate more advanced behaviors.
- Extending agents with external tools through the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**: deploying MCP servers on **ECS or Lambda**, defining tools with **OpenAPI**, and adding **humans in the loop** for review, guidance and oversight.
- **Amazon Q Business**: building an agent that securely interacts with company documents, apps and knowledge sources, plus hands-on **Q Business and Q Apps**, enterprise-grade workflows and resource cleanup.
- **Amazon Q Developer**: a coding partner, debugger and generator of integration scaffolding for AI applications.

## Goal

By the end of the section you can **design, deploy and orchestrate** fully featured agentic systems across **Bedrock, Strands, Agent Squad, MCP and Amazon Q**.
