# 6. Section Intro

This section is the foundation for the rest of the course. It grounds you in the core AI concepts and how they are implemented on AWS, before the advanced architectures and exam-level scenarios.

## What the section covers

- **Amazon Bedrock basics**
  - How Bedrock works and how to explore models in the Bedrock Playground.
  - Fine-tuning foundation models with techniques like LoRA.
  - Multimodal models and pipelines that combine text, images and more.
- **Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**
  - Vector stores and Bedrock Knowledge Bases.
  - Hands-on work with chunking strategies, embedding, optimisation and retrieval techniques.
  - The aim is high-quality RAG systems that work in production.
- **Guardrails and prompt engineering**
  - Bedrock Guardrails and token-level redaction.
  - The anatomy of an effective prompt, prompt best practices and the types of prompts.
  - Mitigating misuse and bias.
  - Structuring prompts consistently with prompt flows and response format templates.
- **Enterprise integration**
  - Enterprise integration patterns.
  - The AWS Well-Architected Generative AI Lens, for how AI systems fit into secure, scalable, real-world architectures.

## Outcome

A solid grounding in generative AI and Amazon Bedrock, ready to build real applications and for the deeper, exam-focused material in later sections.
