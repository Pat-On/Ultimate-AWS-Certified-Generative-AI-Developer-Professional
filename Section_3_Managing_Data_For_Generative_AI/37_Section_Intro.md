# 37. Section Intro

Managing data is a critical part of building high-quality, production-ready generative AI systems. This section covers how to prepare, manipulate, store and optimise the data that powers your models, vector stores and knowledge bases.

## What the section covers

- **Preparing and transforming data**
  - The fundamentals of working with structured data.
  - Transforming and preparing it with Amazon Bedrock's data manipulation features, **SageMaker Data Wrangler** and **AWS Glue**.
  - Hands-on work with **Glue Studio**, **Glue Data Quality** and **Amazon CloudWatch metrics** to monitor data pipelines.
- **Processing text and audio**
  - How **Amazon Transcribe** and **Amazon Comprehend** process audio and text.
  - An in-depth look at entity extraction, sentiment analysis and other pre-processing tasks that improve downstream model performance.
- **Vector databases, the core of AI data architecture**
  - How **Amazon OpenSearch** handles indexing performance, tuning, stability, serverless deployments and multimodal integrations.
  - Relational and NoSQL options and how they fit AI workloads: **Amazon RDS**, **Aurora with pgvector** and **DynamoDB**.
- **Keeping retrieval accurate**
  - Keeping vector stores accurate and fresh.
  - Designing advanced search architectures.
  - Crafting **metadata frameworks** that greatly improve retrieval quality for RAG systems.
- **Storing data efficiently with Amazon S3**
  - Storage classes, lifecycle rules and cross-region replication, so data is secure, cost optimised and highly available.

## Outcome

A complete understanding of the full data pipeline for generative AI: from ingestion and transformation, through indexing and retrieval, to long-term storage.
