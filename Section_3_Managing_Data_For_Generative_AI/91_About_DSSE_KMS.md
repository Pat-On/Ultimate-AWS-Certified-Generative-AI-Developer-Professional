# 91. About DSSE-KMS

A note on **DSSE-KMS**, a newer S3 encryption option you will see in the console during the next hands-on.

- DSSE-KMS is **double encryption based on KMS**, released in **June 2023**.
- It is not taught in depth in the course, as it had not appeared in the exam.
- See lecture 90 for its place among the S3 encryption types.
