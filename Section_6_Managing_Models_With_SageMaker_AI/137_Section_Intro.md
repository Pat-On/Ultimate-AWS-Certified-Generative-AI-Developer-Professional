# 137. Section Intro

A guide to **training, deploying, monitoring and governing foundation models at scale** with **SageMaker AI**, built around the safeguards, observability and automation that production deployments need.

## What the section covers

- The full data lifecycle in SageMaker: **data processing, training and deployment**.
- **Built-in deployment safeguards** for deploying models safely and reliably across environments.
- **Optimizing foundation model deployments**: performance tuning, scalable architectures and special considerations for large language models.
- **SageMaker Ground Truth** for high-quality data labeling.
- **Model Monitor and Clarify** to detect drift, bias and anomalies in model outputs.
- **Model Registry** (versioning strategies) and **lineage tracking**, for full traceability and governance of AI workflows.
- Deploying and optimizing models **at the edge** with **SageMaker Neo and Greengrass**.
- **SageMaker Unified Studio**, a centralized workspace for building and managing generative AI applications.
- **SageMaker Pipelines** for orchestrating automated ML workflows, plus hands-on with **SageMaker JumpStart** to deploy and experiment with a pre-built foundation model.

## Goal

By the end of the section you can manage the **full lifecycle of a foundation model** with SageMaker: from ingestion and training to deployment, monitoring and continuous improvement.
