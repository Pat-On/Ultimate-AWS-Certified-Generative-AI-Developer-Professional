# 304. Intro: Getting Ready for the Exam

The final section is an exam-readiness guide for the **AWS Certified Generative AI Developer Professional** exam. The technical content is done; this section is about the exam itself.

## What the section covers

- **Format and logistics**
  - How long the exam is and how many questions it has.
  - The question types AWS uses, and how scoring works.
- **Signing up**
  - How to register, choose a testing environment, and what you need before you sit down.
- **Question styles**
  - Scenario-based prompts and multi-response items, and how to approach them strategically.
- **Test-taking technique**
  - The most important exam techniques, common pitfalls, and how to allocate time.
- **Continued preparation**
  - Reviewing the practice exam, reinforcing key topics, and using official AWS resources.

## Outcome

Walk into exam day with confidence, having the hands-on skills and the exam-specific preparation.
